# EvlveX: demo photography

**Audience:** theme developers and the store owner.

The theme ships with real footwear photographs as **stand-ins** until EvlveX's own photography is ready ([brand-and-content.md §5](../spec/brand-and-content.md)). Wherever a merchant has not chosen an image, `snippets/evlvex-demo-image.liquid` shows one of these instead of Dawn's clothing placeholder illustrations. This covers:
- image sections (hero, image with text, slideshow, collage, video poster, multirow, footer image);
- product and collection cards, including the sample products shown when no collection is chosen;
- products without media, articles without an image, and story plates.

**Turn them off before launch:** go to Theme settings → **EvlveX** → *Show demo photography*. Empty spots then show the EvlveX monogram plate. Any image you upload replaces the demo photo in that spot straight away.

All images are **CC0 or public domain** (no attribution required, commercial use allowed). They come from Wikimedia Commons, and many were originally published on Unsplash. Product shots were padded to 4:5 by extending their own studio background.
