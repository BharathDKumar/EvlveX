# COMEBACK — Ad copy (Google & Meta)

**For:** whoever sets up EvlveX's Google Ads and Meta (Instagram / Facebook) ads. **Date:** 18 Nov 2026. Every line has been checked against the platform's character limit.

**Message:** COMEBACK is a limited-edition series. These shoes tell the stories of people who got knocked down and came back. Once the series sells out, it won't return.

**Rules for every ad**
- "Limited edition" and "won't return" are true only for these two models in these colorways ([brand-and-content.md §6](../spec/brand-and-content.md)). Never use them for a restocked product.
- Do not claim *handmade*, *handcrafted*, *signed by its artisan* or *every pair numbered* until the brand confirms them (open item 5 in [SPEC.md](../SPEC.md)).
- Images: use the campaign photography (`docs/brand/`, e.g. `assets/evx-sg-full.jpg`, `assets/evx-fs-wide.jpg`). Do **not** use the A Fresh Start product photo with the "Thank You" card: its text is garbled.
- Google does not allow exclamation marks in headlines, words in all caps (other than brand names such as COMEBACK) or repeated punctuation. The copy below follows those rules.
- The price (₹3,435) is left out of the copy on purpose, so the ads stay correct if it changes. Google Shopping shows the live price automatically.

## 2. Meta ads (Instagram & Facebook)

Meta shows about 125 characters of primary text before "…more", so the first sentence of each ad works on its own. Keep headlines under 40 characters and descriptions under 30. Call to action: **Shop now**.

- **Headline** (26/40): COMEBACK · Limited Edition
- **Description** (26/30): Free shipping across India
- **Link:** `https://evlvex.in/collections/all?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=comeback_limited`

### 2.6 Audiences
- **New audiences:** India, age 18–40, interests *sneakers, streetwear, sneaker collecting, running, fitness, self-improvement*. Use ads 2.1–2.3.
- **Retargeting:** people who visited evlvex.in or engaged with @evlvex in the last 30 days. Use ad 2.4. This needs the Meta pixel: install Shopify's **Facebook & Instagram** app and connect the @evlvex account.
- **Starting budget:** a small test, e.g. ₹500–1,000 a day per platform for 14 days. After that, keep the ads with the lowest cost per purchase.
