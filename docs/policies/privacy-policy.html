We process personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under them.

1. What we collect

  • Order and account details: your name, email address, phone number, delivery and billing address, the products you buy and your order history.
  • Payment details: payments are processed by our payment gateway and Shopify. We do not see or store your full card number, CVV or UPI PIN.
  • Certificate details: the name you ask us to register as the owner of your pair, and an optional dedication message.
  • Messages: what you send us by email, contact form or Instagram.
  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser, device type, pages viewed and cookies. See section 7.

2. Why we use it

  • To process, ship and support your order, including returns, exchanges and refunds.
  • To create and maintain the certificate of authenticity for your pair.
  • To answer your questions and complaints.
  • To send you news of new chapters only if you subscribe. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To keep the website secure, prevent fraud and improve it.
  • To meet legal duties, such as tax invoices, accounting records and consumer-protection rules.

We use your data with your consent, which you give when you place an order or subscribe, or where the law allows us to without consent, for example to meet a legal obligation.

3. Your certificate page is public

Every EvlveX pair carries a QR tag that opens a certificate page. The certificate page shows the owner's name and any dedication you ask us to add, and anyone who scans the tag can see it. We show only the name you give us, never your email, phone or address. You can ask us at any time to change the name, show initials only, or remove it, by writing to support@evlvex.in. When a pair is returned, its certificate is cancelled and the name is removed.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the store, and only as much as they need:

  • Shopify, our e-commerce platform, which hosts the store and processes orders.
  • Our payment gateway, which processes payments.
  • Courier and logistics partners, who deliver and collect orders.
  • Email and analytics tools.
  • Government authorities, where the law requires it.

Some providers, including Shopify, may store data on servers outside India, with safeguards that comply with Indian law.

5. How long we keep it

We keep order and invoice records for as long as tax and accounting laws require (up to 8 years). We keep other personal data only as long as we need it for the purposes above. Certificate records are kept while the pair's certificate is active.

6. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct, complete, update or erase it;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it);
  • nominate a person to exercise these rights for you in case of death or incapacity;
  • raise a grievance with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India.

To use these rights, write to support@evlvex.in or to our Grievance Officer (below).

7. Cookies

We use cookies that the store needs to work, such as the cart and checkout. We also use analytics cookies to understand how the site is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but parts of the store, such as the cart, may then not work.

8. Security

We use reasonable security practices to protect your data. These include encrypted (HTTPS) connections, a PCI-DSS compliant checkout, and access limited to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Children

Our store is not meant for children under 18. A child may use it only with the consent and involvement of a parent or guardian.
