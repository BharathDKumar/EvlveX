It is in addition to your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, which this policy does not limit.

1. Returns and size exchanges: 7 days

You can return a pair, or exchange it for another size, within 7 days of delivery.

  • The pair must be unworn (tried on indoors only), clean and undamaged.
  • The original numbered box, dust bag, certificate tag and workshop card must be in the box.
  • Pickup is free. We arrange a reverse pickup from your delivery address. Where reverse pickup is not available at your pin code, we will tell you how to send the pair back and reimburse the courier charge.
  • Size exchanges depend on stock. If your size is not available, we refund you instead.

2. How to start a return or exchange

  1. Email support@evlvex.in within 7 days of delivery with your order number, and tell us whether you want a refund or another size.
  2. We confirm within one working day and schedule the pickup.
  3. When the pair reaches us, we inspect it within 2 working days and tell you the result.

3. Refunds

  • Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI, card, net banking or wallet) within 5–7 working days of the inspection. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
  • We refund the full price you paid, including taxes. Shipping is free, so there is no shipping charge to deduct.
  • When a pair is returned, its certificate is cancelled and the owner's name is removed from its certificate page.

4. Defective, damaged, wrong or not-as-described pairs

If a pair arrives damaged or defective, is not the pair you ordered (wrong model or size), or is not as described on our website, tell us within 48 hours of delivery at support@evlvex.in with your order number and clear photos of the pair and the box. We will collect it free of charge and, as you choose, replace it or refund you in full. The 7-day condition in section 1 does not limit this right. If a manufacturing defect appears later, write to us. We will review it fairly and in line with the law.

5. Late delivery

If we do not deliver within the time stated in our Shipping policy (except for reasons outside our control), you may cancel the order and get a full refund.

6. Cancellations

  • You can cancel an order free of charge until it is dispatched. Email support@evlvex.in with your order number. The refund is made to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.
  • Once an order has been dispatched it can no longer be cancelled, but you can return it under section 1 after delivery.
  • We may cancel an order if a product is unavailable, if there was an obvious pricing or listing error, or if a payment cannot be verified. We will then refund you in full.

7. What we cannot accept

  • Pairs that have been worn outdoors, washed, altered or damaged after delivery.
  • Returns requested more than 7 days after delivery (except under sections 4 and 5).
  • Pairs returned without the certificate tag or workshop card, unless they were missing on delivery.

8. Questions and grievances

Write to support@evlvex.in. If you are not satisfied with our answer, contact our Grievance Officer (details in our Contact information). The Grievance Officer acknowledges complaints within 48 hours and resolves them within one month.
