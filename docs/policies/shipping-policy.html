1. Where we ship

We ship to addresses across India. We do not ship outside India at the moment.

2. Shipping cost

Shipping is free on every order. The price you see is the final price, inclusive of all taxes (MRP).

3. Payment

We accept prepaid orders only: UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and wallets through our secure checkout. Cash on Delivery (COD) is not available.

4. Delivery times

  • Dispatch: within 1–2 working days of your order. Every pair is checked, numbered and registered before it leaves.
  • Delivery: usually 3–7 working days after dispatch, depending on your pin code.
  • Remote areas, the North-East, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, the Andaman & Nicobar Islands and Lakshadweep may take longer.
  • Working days are Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays.

These times are estimates. Weather, strikes, public holidays, courier disruptions and other events outside our control can cause delays. If your order is delayed, we will tell you by email.

5. Tracking

When your order is dispatched we email you, and send an SMS where available, with the courier name and a tracking link.

6. Packaging and insurance

Every pair travels in its numbered box and dust bag, with its certificate tag and workshop card. Shipments are insured until they are delivered to you.

7. On delivery

  • Please check that the outer package is sealed and undamaged. If it is visibly damaged or tampered with, refuse it or note it with the courier, and email us at support@evlvex.in.
  • Tell us within 48 hours if anything is damaged, missing or wrong. See our Refund policy.

8. Failed or refused deliveries

The courier will try to deliver more than once. If a parcel comes back to us because the address was incorrect, nobody was available, or delivery was refused, we will contact you. You can ask us to re-ship it free of charge once, or to refund the order to your original payment method once the parcel is back with us.

9. Address changes

You can change the delivery address until the order is dispatched. Email support@evlvex.in with your order number.

10. Invoice

A GST tax invoice is included in the box and emailed to you.
